Start

Sab se pehle apni assigned lead Google Sheet mein open karo.

Business ko 30 seconds dekho:

  • Website hai ya nahi
  • Social media kaisa hai
  • Business kis type ka hai

Uske baad relevant script use karo.

Lead dekho → Website check karo → Social media check karo → Relevant script → Call → Status + Notes

Yeh guide basic scripts deti hai — starting point hai. Experience ke saath tum khud bhi adapt karogi. Agar customer ki requirement alag ho ya guide mein exact jawab na mile, to ChatGPT ya Claude se help le sakti ho. Neeche ready template hai.

Is Business Ko Kya Pitch Karni Hai?

Upar se business ki situation select karo.

Primary opportunity: Website + Social Media

Sab se pehle website ki conversation start karo.

Opportunity: Website Improvement

Agar social media bhi weak ho: Website + Social Media

Opportunity: Social Media Management

Website pitch force mat karo.

Potential opportunity: AI / Automation Secondary

Agar Situation Different Ho — ChatGPT / Claude

Har call same nahi hoti. Kabhi customer aisi baat karta hai jo is guide mein listed nahi. Tab panic mat karo.

  • Pehle Customer Says section check karo — shayad wahan jawab ho.
  • Phir bhi clear na ho to ChatGPT ya Claude use karo.
  • Neeche wala template copy karo, [ ] wali jagah apni detail likho, paste karo — AI tumhe batayega ke customer ko exactly kya bolna hai.

Important: AI se jo response aaye, usme se price ya technical details khud add mat karo. Agar unsure ho to team se confirm karo.

Assigned To

Jis caller ka naam assigned hai, woh lead usi ki hai. Kisi aur ki lead randomly mat uthao.

Assignment issue ho to conflict use karo aur Notes mein issue likho.

Status Guide

New
Abhi tak lead ko contact nahi kiya.
Contacted
Customer se contact ho gaya, lekin abhi clear next outcome nahi hai.
Follow-up
Customer se dobara contact karna hai. Notes: "Wednesday ko 5 PM call karni hai."
Interested
Customer genuinely interested hai. Notes mein requirement likho.
Not Interested
Customer ne clearly mana kar diya.
Converted
Sale/client confirm ho gaya.
Closed
Lead ka further action close ho gaya.
msg
Lead ke liye message-based communication use karni hai.
powered off
Phone currently switched off / unreachable hai.
conflict
Lead mein assignment, duplicate ya koi internal issue hai. Notes mein issue clearly likho.

Status guess mat karo. Call ke baad actual situation ke according status lagao.

Important information hamesha Notes mein likho.

Interested

Interested → details lo → Notes mein requirement → team ko pass

Follow-up

Follow-up → Notes mein exact reason/time

Not Interested

Not Interested → short note if useful → move to next lead

Yaad Rakho

  • Customer se argue nahi karna.
  • Force nahi karna.
  • Apni taraf se price nahi banana.
  • Technical answer guess nahi karna.
  • Script ko robotic way mein nahi parhna.
  • Customer ki baat zyada sunni hai.
  • Har business ko same pitch nahi deni.
  • Genuine interested lead ko properly note karna hai.
